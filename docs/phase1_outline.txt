======================================================================
PHASE 1: THE REPRODUCTION AND VALIDATION PIPELINE (ASTaUND)
======================================================================

Objective: Successfully mimic the preprocessing and technical 
validation pipeline described in Kamat et al. (2023) Scientific Data 
to isolate cortical neural signatures from surgical tasks.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: DATA QUALITY ANALYSIS (1:1 REPRODUCTION)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Method: Power-based Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) and Motion Ratio (MR).
- SNR Logic: 20 * log10(mean(Intensity) / std(Intensity)).
- SNR Threshold: Valid channels must fall between 80 dB and 140 dB.
- MR Logic: Percentage of the recording free from motion artifacts 
  (spikes/baseline shifts) detected by the hmrR_MotionArtifact function.
- OPTION: Scalp Coupling Index (SCI). Measures the correlation 
  of cardiac oscillations between 690nm and 830nm wavelengths. 

----------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: MODIFIED BEER-LAMBERT LAW (1:1 REPRODUCTION)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Task: Convert Raw Light Intensity to HbO2 and HbR concentrations.
- Specific Constants (PPF): 
    * 6.4 for 690nm
    * 5.8 for 830nm
- Goal: Transform physics-based optical density into biological signals.
- OPTION: Standard Differential Pathlength Factors (DPF). Using age-
  corrected constants instead of the paper-specific PPF values.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: MOTION ARTIFACT REMOVAL (1:1 REPRODUCTION)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Method: Recursive PCA (rPCA).
- Logic: Iteratively identifies and removes the principal components 
  of the signal that account for excessive variance (motion spikes) 
  caused by physical laparoscopic tool use.
- OPTION: Temporal Derivative Distribution Repair (TDDR) or Spline 
  Interpolation. Alternative algorithms for smoothing motion spikes.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: SYSTEMIC NOISE REGRESSION (1:1 REPRODUCTION)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Method: Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Regression.
- Regressors: 8mm Short-Separation Channels.
- Goal: Subtract physiological noise (heart rate, scalp blood flow) 
  measured by the short channels from the 30-40mm cortical channels.
- OPTION: Adaptive Filtering (RLS/LMS). A dynamic method of noise 
  cancellation that adjusts to physiological changes in real-time.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: GLM & TECHNICAL VALIDATION (1:1 REPRODUCTION)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Model: General Linear Model (GLM) with Gaussian Basis Functions.
- Purpose: Extract the flexible Hemodynamic Response Function (HRF) 
  for the surgical pattern cutting task.
- Metric: Contrast-to-Noise Ratio (CNR).
- Formula: |μ_expert - μ_novice| / sqrt(σ²_expert + σ²_novice).
- OPTION: Canonical HRF. Using a fixed "double-gamma" function instead 
  of flexible Gaussian Basis Functions.
